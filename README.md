## World Theme
<img width="1280" height="720" alt="Gargantia" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/05e5a10f-98da-4a09-849e-7e63da3377ac" />
The world theme of Gargantia on the Verdurous Planet is best described as post-apocalyptic maritime science fiction with a focus on human civilization, survival, and everyday life. It presents a future where the planet has undergone drastic environmental changes, leaving most of its surface covered by an enormous ocean. As a result, humanity has adapted to these conditions by establishing floating civilizations made up of interconnected ships, creating communities that function almost like small moving cities.

## The Everyday Life of Gargantia 

The everyday life in Gargantia on the Verdurous Planet revolves around a maritime lifestyle where people live, work, and establish communities aboard massive interconnected ships. Since most of the planet is covered by water, their daily activities and occupations are heavily influenced by the ocean and the limited resources available to them.
